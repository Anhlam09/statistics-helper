# About

> A free, friendly guide to choosing and running statistical tests in clinical research.

## Hi, I'm Anh Lam

I made Biostat Test Guide because "which test should I use?" is one of the most common questions in clinical research, and the answers are spread across textbooks, forum threads, and software manuals. This site puts them in one place, in plain language, with a cup of coffee and a few plush friends for company.

## What you'll find here

- **62 lessons**, one per test or method. Each one says when to use it, which assumptions to check, and how to read and report the result.
- **A clinical example in every lesson**, with code you can run in **R** and **Python**.
- **A "Which test?" decision tree**, a claw machine, and a "describe your study" box to help you find the right lesson.
- **Interactive calculators** for common tests and sample-size questions.

The site is free to use and needs no sign-up. It also works on phones.

## Please note

<div class="callout-warn"><strong>Educational resource.</strong> Always pre-specify your analysis and consult a statistician for trial work.</div>

This site teaches the reasoning behind common analyses. It does not replace a statistical analysis plan, a protocol review, or advice from a qualified statistician about your own study.
